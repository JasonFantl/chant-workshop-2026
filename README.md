# Chant workshop results (Nov 20, 2026)

At a chant workshop in Cambridge hosted by AIST (AI Safety Team), eight AI safety researchers chanted 16 rally chants together, then each scored them privately from 1 to 5 on ease, fun and message. Nine of the chants had been used at AI rallies in 2026; seven were written at the workshop.

## Files

- [`AI-chants-memo.pdf`](AI-chants-memo.pdf): two-page memo with findings, limitations, the scoring key and every chant's average scores.
- [`Chant_Workshop_Scores.xlsx`](Chant_Workshop_Scores.xlsx): the raw data.
  - **Summary:** average scores per chant.
  - **Scores:** every scorecard's score for every chant. Only rows marked Counted = Yes go into the averages.
  - **Comments:** participants' written comments.
  - **Read me:** how the scorecards were transcribed.

The scorers were a small, selected group (all AI safety researchers and past protesters), so the scores reflect their views, not the public's. The memo's Limitations section covers this and other caveats.
